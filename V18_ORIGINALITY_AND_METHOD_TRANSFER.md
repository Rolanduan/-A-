| General research concern seen in comparison | Independent V18 formulation | What is deliberately *not* transferred |
|---|---|---|
| Data gaps can affect which winners are defensible | An evidence **outer horizon** formed from our actual ACS E/M stress and OSM access-unknown bounds; sufficient exclusion and conservative non-exclusion labels | Their global sixteen-city data, TOPSIS point/interval score functions, and claims that an envelope gives an objective win probability |

## Sources of our distinctiveness

1. Each score is computed from a real municipal system with audited government demographic files, GIS geography and legally routable OSM pedestrian edges. The unit of inference remains the spatially weighted city, never a global index imputed from heterogeneous statistics.
2. Our uncertainty decomposition is mechanism-specific: official ACS marginal sampling stress and OSM feature-eligibility ignorance enter the 60/40 score **through their native mathematical contributions**. The rectangular outer envelope is explicitly overconservative and possibly non-jointly-feasible.
3. Verification priority is framed as **conditional upper-bound influence**, not an unobserved "expected value of information" or guaranteed acquisition payoff.
4. Our preference alternatives work in an additive six-dimensional GIS representation and use analytic pairwise line intersections. They are not empirically elicited preferences, and they do not change the chosen reference winner unless the author explicitly changes the declared decision policy in a separate analysis.
5. The systems-science mechanism remains: spatial hierarchy, cross-boundary permeability, resource complementarity, threshold sensitivity and research feedback versus unidentifiable actual urban feedback.

## Independent creation, attribution and admissibility

All new V18 production scripts (`evidence_frontier.py`, `preference_paths.py`, `decision_atlas.py`, `decision_evidence_review.py`, `decision_release.py`) were authored as independent implementations of well-known concepts using our own declared data structures and mathematical requirements. No code from another HiMCM team was imported. The methodology itself does not become "original mathematics" by renaming standard concepts; full scholarly claims should cite foundational sources for interval analysis, multicriteria decision making, and transparent data provenance when those methods are materially used.

Because the compared repository relates to a live 2026 contest, formally competing teams should separately consider the event's originality rules. This particular project is an independent research effort, not a representation that COMAP accepted or approved another team's material.

## Epistemic safeguards

- Published marginal ACS MOEs are not a simultaneous city-by-city confidence region.
- The score outer horizon can include combinations forbidden by correlated census estimates or spatial constraints; `not_excluded_by_outer_bound` is not a "genuine probability to win".
- An OSM tag of public access is not verified eligibility to enter a specific library or park after school; unknown access is not admitted into the reference automatically.
- An analyst-defined household family is not a survey of student preferences; the policy winner for the original M1 remains the primary ranking.
- No code-writing test substitutes for actual collection of 28 census originals, 12 OSM source records, live full GIS computations, external data comparisons and a human science review.
