# V19.1 — GIS Atlas and Figure Discussion Contract

Status: **UPGRADE PATCH / additive only**. This file upgrades V19. It does not delete, replace, or invalidate any existing V19 GIS design files.

## 1. Non-negotiable preservation rule

All GIS maps planned in V19 shall be preserved in one of three layers:

1. **Main-paper GIS figures**: a small editorial subset used inside the 25-page paper.
2. **Complete GIS atlas**: the full set of city-level and algorithm-level GIS maps.
3. **GIS data-layer outputs**: per-city, per-threshold, and per-algorithm exports used for audit and reproducibility.

No GIS class may be dropped merely because the main paper has page pressure.

## 2. Required GIS figure families

The final V19 implementation shall preserve or generate the following GIS figure families:

### Family A — spatial frame and population basis
- **GIS-A1**: US locator map of the 12 frozen cities.
- **GIS-A2**: city PLACE boundary + TRACT overlay map.
- **GIS-A3**: youth-proxy (ACS 15–17) tract choropleth.
- **GIS-A4**: spatial interpolation / geographic matching diagnostic map.

### Family B — current opportunity geography
- **GIS-B1**: real OSM pedestrian graph over audited city.
- **GIS-B2**: eligible / restricted / unknown facilities map.
- **GIS-B3**: routed access map (selected OD paths).
- **GIS-B4**: tract opportunity choropleth under the frozen M1 access kernel.
- **GIS-B5**: 10/20/30-minute real-network reach corridor maps.
- **GIS-B6**: cross-boundary access and city-edge permeability map.

### Family C — algorithm I / graph-routing GIS outputs
- **GIS-C1**: directed graph with source/target node snapping diagnostics.
- **GIS-C2**: route mismatch / directionality diagnostic map.
- **GIS-C3**: truncated-search coverage subnetwork map.

### Family D — algorithm II / facility optimization GIS outputs
- **GIS-D1**: candidate facility layer.
- **GIS-D2**: selected hypothetical facility placements for a fixed K.
- **GIS-D3**: before/after tract opportunity improvement map.
- **GIS-D4**: underserved-tract improvement emphasis map.

### Family E — algorithm III / fairness and resilience GIS outputs
- **GIS-E1**: lowest-opportunity tracts highlight map.
- **GIS-E2**: tail-fairness geographic concentration map.
- **GIS-E3**: edge-removal impact / vulnerable corridor map.
- **GIS-E4**: opportunity loss after hypothetical closure map.

### Family F — comparative multi-city atlas
- **GIS-F1**: 12-city small multiples of tract opportunity.
- **GIS-F2**: 12-city small multiples of pedestrian network density / routing frame.
- **GIS-F3**: 12-city small multiples for one fixed threshold (e.g., 20-minute reach corridor or normalized opportunity layer).

## 3. Detailed discussion requirement

Every GIS figure must have a matching discussion block. Each block shall include:

1. **What the map shows** — exact geometry layers and variables.
2. **Why it matters** — the scientific claim supported.
3. **How it is computed** — source files, CRS, snapping logic, thresholds, aggregation.
4. **What must not be over-interpreted** — unknown facility eligibility, age proxy, entry-point uncertainty, hypothetical interventions, etc.
5. **Link to results** — which equation / CSV / manuscript paragraph uses it.

## 5. Main-paper GIS selection rule

The paper may select only a compact subset, but it must still preserve atlas completeness.

Recommended main-paper GIS subset:
- GIS-A2/A3 combined compactly
- GIS-B4 (one audited city opportunity map)
- GIS-B5 or GIS-B6 (true network access / permeability)
- GIS-D3 or GIS-D4 (optimization effect)
- GIS-E3 or GIS-E4 (resilience / vulnerability)

The rest go to the complete atlas.

## 6. Technical quality rules

Every GIS map must satisfy:
- real CRS recorded;
- north arrow and scale bar where meaningful;
- legend with units;
- frozen city GEOID in caption or filename;
- actual network source timestamp;
- no Euclidean circle mislabeled as a network isochrone;
- no unknown-access facility silently merged with public-access facility;
- no unexplained normalization.

## 7. Acceptance gate

If V19 later executes with real data, the GIS atlas is considered complete only if:
- all 12 frozen cities appear in the atlas manifest;
- each city has at least one Family A and one Family B map;
- at least one audited city has Families C, D, and E rendered;
- figure discussion records exist for every GIS figure;
- source hashes and run_id are linked.

This file is a preservation-and-discussion upgrade only. It does not claim that the real GIS maps have already been generated.
